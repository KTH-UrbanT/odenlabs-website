DASHER helps historic buildings use less energy without harming their cultural heritage values or indoor environment. Using building data, digital tools and simulations, the project identifies sensitive and cost-effective measures for each building in Gamla stan, Stockholm. The longer-term aim is a scalable method and clear guidance that authorities can use when applying EU energy requirements in protected historic areas across Sweden.

KTH coordinates the project and contributes established KTH tools. Partners: AB Stadsholmen, Aktea Energy, ElectriCITY, the City of Stockholm and Uppsala University.
