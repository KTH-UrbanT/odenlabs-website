MUBES is a bottom-up, physics-based UBEM tool. It combines data from public sources into dynamic building-physics models and calibrates the simulations against measured energy use. The project used it to study how level of detail, thermal zoning and shading affect results at district and city scale.

The tool can be used to analyse the current energy performance of a building stock, map the system effects of large-scale renovation, fill gaps in missing data with calibrated simulations, and test operating strategies that ease the load on district heating and power grids.

Funded by the Swedish Energy Agency's E2B2 programme (project 46896-1).
