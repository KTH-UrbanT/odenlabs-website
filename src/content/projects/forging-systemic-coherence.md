Single urban experiments rarely add up to wider system innovation. This project explores how a portfolio approach can connect them and give transitions a direction. It draws on theories of change in social systems, such as the multi-level perspective, leverage points and resilience thinking, to support the design, sensemaking and monitoring of transformative portfolios. It also looks at how data-based digital tools can make portfolios a platform for strategic learning in cities.

The study is transdisciplinary. It works with selected Swedish municipalities and two intermediaries: the strategic innovation programme Viable Cities and the Strategic Innovation Unit of UNDP. The results feed into a toolbox for the transformative portfolio approach, with guidelines grounded in theory and illustrated with practical examples.

Led by Kateryna Pereverza, funded by a Formas grant for early-career researchers (2022-01376), with Olga Kordas (KTH) and Harald Rohracher (Linköping University) as participating researchers.
