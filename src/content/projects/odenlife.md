OdenLife adapted technology from KTH and LocalLife to structured energy renovation and power measures in the housing stock of Svenska Bostäder, a municipal housing company in Stockholm. The result was a system that shows energy managers current energy and power use, helps them choose measures across the portfolio and follow up their effects, and lets the company offer its flexibility through an aggregator service tested against Svenska kraftnät.

Led by Oleksii Pasichnyi with Svenska Bostäder and LocalLife Sweden. Funded by Vinnova's call Innovations for reduced electricity use (2022-03307).
