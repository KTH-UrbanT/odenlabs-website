The second phase of an E2B2 project led by Hossein Shahrokni. It developed calculation models for more building types and measures, building on the investment tools of the Bebo and Belok networks, and a public visualisation of the results, the energy map of Stockholm. It also looked at how the method could scale to other cities, together with the City of Stockholm and Lassila & Tikanoja.

Funded by the Swedish Energy Agency's E2B2 programme (project 40846-2).
