The project asks how building energy data can be used to scale up sustainable transitions in cities. It aims to advance energy simulation of buildings at district, city and national scale, improve how the built environment is represented in data, and develop and pilot decision-support tools with municipalities and property owners in different urban contexts.

It builds on the ODEN urban energy data platform and uses big data and AI/ML to improve predictions of energy performance and to segment building stocks for targeted measures. Research visits to the National University of Singapore, New York University and the Technical University of Madrid build a research network around the work.

Led by Oleksii Pasichnyi, funded by a Formas career grant for early-career researchers (2024-00465).
