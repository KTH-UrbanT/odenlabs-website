Kateryna researches urban transition governance, drawing on transition studies, future studies, complex systems theory and transdisciplinarity. She developed the concept of transformative portfolio-based approaches: systemic, long-term ways of steering multi-actor and multi-level urban transitions beyond single projects. Her work covers multi-level governance configurations, transition intermediaries, the reframing of challenges, and participatory backcasting for energy systems.

She leads the Formas-funded projects Forging systemic coherence, on transformative portfolios for urban transition governance, and Climate-neutral and inclusive communities – Skellefteå, which explored mobility transitions in Nordic winter cities. She is leading researcher in the Digital Energy Communities project. She has worked with the [Viable Cities](https://viablecities.se/en/) programme, the [Strategic Innovation Unit of UNDP](https://medium.com/@undp.innovation) and the [Agirre Lehendakaria Center](https://www.agirrecenter.eus/en/) in the Basque Country.

She received her PhD in Industrial Ecology from KTH in 2019 and was a postdoctoral researcher embedded in Viable Cities from 2019 to 2021. Since 2016 she has developed and taught the KTH course Transdisciplinary Approaches for System Innovations, and she co-edits the Medium series Meaningful Collaborations for System Transformations.
