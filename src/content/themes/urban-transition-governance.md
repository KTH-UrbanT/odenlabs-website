---
title: Urban transition governance
summary: How municipalities, national programmes and other actors steer climate transitions across levels of governance, and how futures methods, data and models can support that work.
order: 5
---

We study how cities plan, steer and follow up their transition to climate neutrality, and how decision support, energy models and data analytics can be built into that governance. Topics include multi-level governance arrangements and the role of transition intermediaries, transformative portfolio approaches and the reframing of challenges, future studies, scenario development and participatory backcasting for long-term strategic planning, and the capacities that municipalities and other public organisations need to drive change.

The aim is to help cities understand where they stand, plan next steps and monitor progress transparently.
