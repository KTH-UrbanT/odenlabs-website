We study how cities plan, steer and follow up their transition to climate neutrality, and how decision support, energy models and data analytics can be built into that governance. Topics include transformative portfolio approaches, backcasting and long-term strategic planning, participatory and transdisciplinary methods, and the capacities that municipalities and other public organisations need to drive change.

The aim is to help cities understand where they stand, plan next steps and monitor progress transparently.
