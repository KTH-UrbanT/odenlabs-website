---
title: Urban building energy modelling
summary: Energy models for every building in a city, used to estimate energy use and solar potential and to test renovation and climate scenarios.
order: 1
---

We build a simple energy model for each building, based on its walls, windows, heating system and use, rather than one average for the area. Running these models for thousands of buildings at once gives energy use for a block, a district or a whole city.

The models are used to answer "what if?" questions: the effect of renovations, added insulation or a warmer climate on energy use and cost. The same city-scale approach is used to estimate the potential for solar PV on buildings.

Our open-source framework MUBES is available on GitHub: https://github.com/KTH-UrbanT/MUBES_UBEM
