## Glossary

- maintainer — any person with rights to merge changes that publish to the live site. NOT group member (members send updates to a maintainer but cannot publish) and NOT KTH IT (they run the server, not the site).
- visitor — anyone who reads the public site in a browser: prospective students and postdocs, funders, collaborators, industry partners, academic peers and the public. NOT group member (members are the subject of pages, not an audience; their internal space is Notion).
